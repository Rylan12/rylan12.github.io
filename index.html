About Me

I’m a recent graduate from Yale University with a degree in electrical engineering and computer science.

In my free time, I maintain Homebrew, a package manager for macOS and Linux. I served on Homebrew’s technical steering committee from 2021 to 2025.

Some of my interests include ASIC/SoC/VLSI design, railroad engineering (interlocking/signal design in particular), embedded systems, and software development/programming.

At Yale, I was involved in technical theater through the Yale Dramatic Association. There, I explored sound engineering, carpentry, and electrician work. I served as the Production Officer of the company from 2023 to 2024, where I oversaw the company's six-show season.
